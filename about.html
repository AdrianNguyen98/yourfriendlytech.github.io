The Story Behind

01 / The Genesis

54 Park Road,
Cabramatta.

Suite 107, Level 1 · Est. 2022

Lee Nguyen Lab didn’t begin as a corporate storefront. In 2022, it started with a personal obsession with hardware, an illuminated microscope, a soldering iron, and a simple frustration: watching perfectly salvageable devices get written off simply because the rest of the industry found it easier to say, “it’s unfixable, just replace it.”

From our workshop at Suite 107, Level 1/54 Park Rd in Cabramatta, we set out with a clear purpose—to bring genuine craftsmanship, technical honesty, and component-level mastery back to device repair.

02 / The Philosophy

More Than Just Swapping Parts

When a phone screen shatters, a laptop refuses to boot, or an iPad suffers water damage, it is never just about the broken glass. It is about irrecoverable photos, crucial business documents, school projects, and the lifeline people depend on every day.

We realized early on that Western Sydney didn’t need another kiosk doing hasty part swaps. The community needed an authentic technician who understood what was happening at the logic board level. While standard counters turn away complex board failures, we get under the microscope—diagnosing micro-soldering faults, rebuilding damaged motherboard traces, and recovering liquid-damaged hardware.

“If a device only needs a five-minute port clean rather than an expensive socket replacement, that is exactly what we tell you. If a repair is not economically viable, we share straightforward advice before you spend a single dollar.”

03 / The Benchmark

The Lab Standard

Precision engineering protocols applied to every single device that reaches our bench.

01

Respecting What’s Inside

The shields, screws, brackets, and factory water-resistant seals that clients never see are put back in place with meticulous precision.

02

On-the-Spot Turnaround

We know being without your device disrupts your day, so standard screen and battery services are completed right before your eyes in around 30 minutes.

03

Transparent Options

We offer transparent part tiers—ranging from budget-friendly aftermarket components to OEM-spec displays and genuine original packs.

04

Tested & Warranted

Every device undergoes rigorous post-repair quality checks and leaves our bench backed by up to 6 months of comprehensive warranty.

04 / Our Roots

Dedicated to Our Community

Since 2022, we’ve proudly supported locals across Cabramatta and Greater Sydney, alongside providing dependable fleet support for neighborhood schools and small businesses.

Whether your daily driver took a heavy drop or suddenly went silent, bring it by the bench. We’ll inspect it under the scope and treat your gear with the care it deserves.
