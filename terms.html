Terms & Conditions

Transparent protocols for hardware diagnostics, 6-month warranties, client privacy, and lab service agreements.

Effective Edition: 2026 · Jurisdiction: New South Wales, Australia · Australian Consumer Law (ACL) Compliant ·
01.

Diagnostic Assessment, Quotations & Authorization

Scope of Work

1.1 Diagnostic Triage: Prior to undertaking physical repairs, our Sydney workshop technicians perform a preliminary hardware diagnostic. Where obvious faults exist (such as shattered digitizers or degraded battery cells), itemized pricing is provided upfront using our standard fee schedule.

1.2 Latent Hardware & Board Faults: Electronic devices brought in post-impact or after liquid exposure frequently harbor internal micro-fractures, lifted circuit traces, or short-circuited surface-mount capacitors. Should our microscopic bench inspection reveal secondary faults outside the initial quote, we pause work and contact the client for explicit authorization prior to commencing additional tasks.

1.3 Ultrasonic & Deoxidation Bench Work: Complex liquid damage recovery requires multi-stage ultrasonic chemical cleaning, thermal imaging, and trace re-bridging. A non-refundable diagnostic triage fee may apply to cover chemical bench supplies and micro-inspection labor, which is completely credited towards the total invoice upon successful repair.

02.

Comprehensive Hardware Warranty Policy

Up to 6 Months

2.1 Coverage Scope: Lee Nguyen Lab warrants all installed replacement hardware components and bench workmanship against defects in material and assembly for a period of up to six (6) months from the date of client collection, as specified on your invoice:

  • Genuine Service Packs & Premium OEM-Spec Modules: Covered by our full 6-Month Comprehensive Warranty (touch responsiveness, OLED display uniformity, battery health retention, port connectivity).
  • Aftermarket Budget Modules: Covered by a 90-Day Functional Warranty against component defects.
  • Logic Board Micro-Soldering Repairs: Covered by a 90-Day Warranty on the specific IC chips, trace jumpers, or soldered power rails restored.

2.2 What Is Excluded: Warranty coverage does not apply if the device experiences subsequent accidental damage post-handover, including:

  • Fresh physical drops resulting in cracked glass, hairline fissures, or internal OLED bleeding / black ink spots.
  • New liquid ingress, moisture exposure, or chemical deoxidation.
  • Unauthorized disassembly, modification, or tamper seals broken by any third-party kiosk or repairer.
  • Operating system software corruption, unauthorized jailbreaks, or user password lockouts unrelated to our physical repair.
03.

Data Confidentiality, Privacy & Client Backups

Zero Inspection Policy

3.1 Strict Client Privacy: We hold personal privacy as an absolute standard. Technicians will never browse, inspect, transfer, or disclose any customer files, personal photos, text messages, passwords, or personal applications during the repair process. Passcodes provided for post-repair diagnostic verification (camera, microphone, speaker, touchscreen calibration) are utilized strictly for functional testing.

3.2 Backup Responsibility: While routine screen, battery, and charging port replacements do not intentionally alter flash storage, complex hardware triage, thermal logic board rework, or power rail surges carry inherent electrical risks. Clients are strongly urged to perform an independent cloud (iCloud / Google Drive) or computer backup prior to surrendering devices for service.

3.3 Biometric & Telemetry Preservation: Wherever technically viable, our laboratory utilizes advanced hardware serial programmer tools to transfer TrueTone serialization, ambient light calibration, and original Face ID / Touch ID sensors to your new replacement hardware.

04.

Workshop Turnaround, Pickup & Storage Policy

Cabramatta Lab Bench

4.1 Turnaround Timelines: Standard mobile screen replacements and battery swaps are ordinarily completed on the spot in approximately 30–45 minutes. Micro-soldering, board jumper recovery, and liquid ultrasonic treatments require between 24 to 72 hours to ensure comprehensive post-thermal burn-in validation.

4.2 Notification & Verification: Upon bench completion, clients receive automated confirmation via SMS or email. The physical collection of any repaired device requires presentation of the workshop claim ticket, matching identification, or verified booking phone number.

4.3 Uncollected Hardware Policy: Devices not collected within 60 days of completion notification will incur reasonable storage fees, and after 90 days may be recycled or disposed of in strict accordance with the New South Wales Uncollected Goods Act 1995 to recover outstanding parts and labor costs.

05.

Custom Phone Cases & UV Print Studio Terms

UV Laser Studio

5.1 Custom Artwork Authorization: Clients uploading custom images, photographs, or corporate graphics into our interactive print studio warrant that they possess all required legal rights, copyright permissions, or licenses to reproduce the supplied content.

5.2 Color Rendering & Alignment: While our laboratory employs high-definition direct UV inkjet printers on 9H tempered glass and frosted surfaces, slight variances may occur between on-screen RGB digital mockups and final physical CMYK+White UV cured prints due to display color profiles and optical glass refraction.

5.3 Print Longevity Guarantee: Our cured UV inks are scratch-resistant, solvent-tested, and guaranteed against peeling or sunlight UV degradation under standard everyday usage. Customized personalized cases are manufactured bespoke to client specifications and cannot be refunded solely for change of mind.
